# OraCare — product brief (current direction, Oct 2026)

## What it is

A family oral-care app for daily prevention at home. It offers short lessons
for each life stage, an AI-guided photo diary, reminders and check-up nudges,
and the user's own oral-health history to share with their dentist.

Pregnancy is the first full flow; kids, partner, adults and 60+ come later.
It is no longer a women's or "hormonal life stages" app.

## AI

The AI guides the photo only: angle, lighting, sharpness, and tips to retake.
Photos are saved and shown side by side over time, so the user or their
dentist can compare them.

The AI does **not** interpret, score, flag inflammation or diagnose — that
would make it a medical device under EU MDR (class IIa). A certified AI scan
is the long-term vision (year 3+) and can appear only as "coming later",
never as results.

## Score

The Oral Health Score and all per-area scores are removed. Show progress the
user creates instead: check-ins done, photos taken this month, next check-up
due. A gentle weekly rhythm is fine; no daily streak pressure. (Proposal,
open to adjustment.)

## Dentist

Dr. Viktorija Artjomenko (general dentist, Dental Victory, 20+ years) is the
medical advisor. Education content shows a "Reviewed by a dentist" marker ("Dentist-reviewed" on small cards).
She doesn't see users' data; users share their history with their own
dentist if they choose.

Note: as of 2026-10-10 she has not yet reviewed the prototype's tips. The
marker is a design element; the honest answer if asked is "she'll review all
content before launch".

## Business model (decided)

- Insurers pay first: €1 per member a month via employer health insurance.
  Insured users activate through their insurer (exact method open).
- People without cover get **OraCare Plus**: 7-day free trial, then
  €24.99/year or €2.99/month.
- Both include family sharing (partner invite, kids' profiles).
- No free tier for now. Don't call the app "free"; "free trial" is fine.
- The insurer sees only activation and combined usage numbers, never photos
  or health data.

## What the demo must show

The pregnancy flow working end to end:

Welcome → Who is this profile for? → Which fits you best? → pregnancy
questions → dashboard (no score) → photo guide → capture → saved photo with
side-by-side comparison → care plan (no "your scan indicates") → profile
(no score).

Priority: nothing contradicts the pitch — no AI results, no scores, no €4.99
Premium, no "women's" or "hormonal".

New screens like insurer activation, the Plus paywall, consent and app lock
come after the event (11 Oct 2026, investors may scan the QR code on the last
slide).
