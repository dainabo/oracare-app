Why gums bleed more in the 2nd trimester

Reviewed by a dentist

What’s happening

Pregnancy hormones increase blood flow to your gums and make them react more to plaque. Many women notice bleeding when brushing or flossing, most often in the second trimester.

What helps

Brush twice a day with a soft brush, floss gently every day, and don’t skip your check-up. The second trimester is a good time for a dental cleaning.

When to see your dentist

If bleeding is heavy or doesn’t improve, if your gums are very swollen or painful, or if a tooth feels loose.
