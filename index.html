Rinse with alcohol-free antibacterial mouthwash

Medium Priority Ongoing
Manage dry mouth with hydration + xylitol
Drink at least 8–10 cups of water daily. Dry mouth reduces saliva, which protects against cavities. Xylitol gum stimulates saliva safely during pregnancy and inhibits bacteria growth.

Preventive As needed
After vomiting, wait 30 min before brushing
Morning sickness exposes teeth to stomach acid. Brushing immediately can spread acid and erode enamel. Instead, rinse with water or a baking soda solution (1 tsp in 8 oz water) and wait 30 minutes.

Nutrition Daily
Increase Vitamin C & calcium-rich foods
Vitamin C supports gum tissue repair and reduces inflammation. Aim for 85mg/day from citrus, peppers, or strawberries. Calcium protects enamel — dairy, almonds, and leafy greens are great sources.
