BRAND

Brand

Color lockup on light surfaces (Welcome screen) · White lockup on dark/teal surfaces (Splash screen)

OraCare is a women's oral health companion that adapts to hormonal life stages — starting with pregnancy. It pairs an AI smile scan with trimester-aware guidance, turning clinical oral-health advice into a calm, personal daily routine.

Calm over clinical

Health information presented with warmth, not alarm — even when flagging a risk.

Personalize by stage

Guidance changes with trimester and symptoms, never generic.

One primary action per screen

Every screen has a clear next step, not competing calls to action.

Earn trust with restraint

No fake urgency, no invented stats — accuracy over persuasion.

Icons

Two registers, used deliberately: emoji for warm, illustrative moments (life stages, symptoms, quick actions, tips & recommendations), and hand-drawn line icons (1.5–2px stroke, rounded caps/joins, currentColor) for functional UI chrome. Every glyph below is pulled from the prototype — nothing invented.
